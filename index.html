Built for a real business

Brikline — AI operations platform for residential construction.

Built and deployed by Tensra AI for a residential homebuilder. Brikline is one example of what we build — the same approach applies to any business where teams spend too much time moving information between people, documents and software.

Production System Residential Homebuilder Construction

Brikline connects the day-to-day operations of a construction business into one AI-enabled system — from incoming emails and supplier quotes to procurement, invoices, scheduling, site operations and client communication.

  • AI email classification into leads, supplier quotes, invoices and general correspondence
  • Leads → projects, with client details and scope captured automatically
  • Supplier RFQs, quote comparison and purchase orders
  • Supplier invoices matched against purchase orders, synced with Xero
  • Cash-flow dashboard with 7/30/60-day forecasting
  • 189-activity construction schedule with dependency-aware rescheduling
  • Daily site logs with photos and an internal-only flag for sensitive notes
  • A private client portal showing progress without requiring a login
  • An AI assistant staff can ask real questions of the live project data
